[footnote 18] Energy consumption and energy efficiency values are based on the ENERGY STAR Program Requirements for Computers, including the max energy allowance for iMac. For more information, visit www.energystar.gov. iMac is tested with the 143W Power Adapter, AC Cable (1m), and DC Cable (2m).
- Off: Lowest power mode of the system. System is shut down.
- Sleep: Low power state that is entered automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity (default), or by selecting Sleep from the Apple menu. Wake for network access enabled.
- Idle-Display on: System is on and has completed loading macOS. Connected to Wi-Fi.
- Power adapter, no-load: Condition in which the 143W Power Adapter is connected to AC power, but not connected to the system.
- Power adapter efficiency: Average of the 143W Power Adapter measured efficiency when tested at 100 percent, 75 percent, 50 percent, and 25 percent of rated output current.
